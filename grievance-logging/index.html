Behind the scenes

From a hello to a ticket in your system

Four quiet steps happen during one ordinary phone call.

  1. 1

    The citizen calls

    A normal phone call to your grievance number. No app download, no login, no form.

  2. 2

    Details become data

    The agent’s plain questions capture who, what, when and where as clean, structured fields.

  3. 3

    Checked & confirmed

    Every detail is read back to the caller and confirmed out loud before anything is filed.

  4. 4

    Ticket lands in your CRM

    A validated, trackable ticket is pushed straight into your existing complaint or ticketing system.

Callrolin connects through secure APIs to the helpdesk you already run — commercial tools like Zendesk or Freshdesk, government grievance portals, or a custom CRM. No rip-and-replace.

The moment that matters

It ends with a number, not a shrug.

Before the call ends, the agent reads the reference number back, slowly and clearly, so the caller can write it down. That single habit changes everything: the grievance now has an identity the citizen can quote, track and follow up on.

Optional: an SMS carrying the same reference number follows the call, for citizens who want it in writing.
