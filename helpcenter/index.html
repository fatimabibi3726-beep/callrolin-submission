How it works

Three quiet steps between question and answer.

The pattern is called Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). In plain terms: the agent may only speak from what it can find in your documents.

  1. 1

    Your documents go in

    Policy manuals, fee schedules, rulebooks and FAQs are uploaded into a private knowledge base that belongs to your institution alone.

  2. 2

    Every clause becomes findable

    Content is organised and indexed so any section — down to a single clause — can be located within seconds during a live call.

  3. 3

    Exact answers on the call

    When someone asks, the agent finds the governing passage, reads it back clearly, and names the document and clause it came from.

If the answer isn’t supported by your documents, the agent says so plainly instead of guessing.

Caller asks

“My fixed deposit matures on a public holiday. When do I receive the funds?”

Agent reads back

“Maturity proceeds are credited on the next working day. Value dating runs from the original maturity date, so no interest is lost for the holiday.”

Deposit Terms & Conditions · Cl. 9.2

“Where maturity falls on a holiday, proceeds shall be credited on the following business day, with value dating from the original maturity date.”
Deposit Terms & Conditions, Cl. 9.2 · v3.1, Mar 2026

Caller asks
